## The immediate next task: fix ep2's 8 silent demos + all pitches, re-render, verify

### Root cause (confirmed by render diffs + online research)
The 15 audible demos have `F1 Freq` ≈ 1.0; the 8 silent ones have `F1 Freq` ≤ 0.53 **and/or** a filter-envelope that closes the cutoff below the note. The two silent-patch killers:
1. **`AEG1 Sustain = 0` on a held/drone note** → note dies after Decay.
2. **Base `F1 Freq` too low + `F1 Freq < Env` positive + `FEG1 Sustain = 0`** → cutoff opens on attack then settles *below the fundamental* → silence. The audible steady-state cutoff = `base + (F1 Freq<Env × FEG Sustain)`; keep it above the note's fundamental.
3. Also watch `OSC Balance` routing into an OFF filter, and `F1 To F2` serial dead-ends.

### `F1 Freq` is NORMALIZED 0–1 (measured via OSC):
`0.3→221Hz · 0.45→598Hz · 0.5→834Hz · 0.6→1.6k · 0.7→3.1k · 0.8→5.9k · 0.9→11.4k · 1.0→22k`

### The 8 silent demos to rebuild
`an-slope-12-vs-24`, `an-drive-sym-vs-asym`, `an-error-drift`, `an-unison-supersaw` (the 4 A/B), plus `an-digeridoo-drone`, `an-reese-final`, `an-filter-env-vs-amp-env`, and `an-303-reese-final` (concat morph — depends on `an-reese-final`).

### Fix recipes (from research — see `docs/podcast-harness/` or the research agent output)
- **Golden "always sounds" default:** `OSC1 On, Shape=Saw, Balance=0.5, F1 On, F1 Type=LP24, F1 Freq=0.6, F1 Res=0.2, AEG1 A=0.001 D=0.3 S=0.8 R=0.2`.
- **Filter-env demo:** base `F1 Freq=0.35`, `F1 Freq<Env=+0.5`, `FEG1 A=0.001 D=0.8 S=0.25`, `F1 Res=0.4`, `AEG1 S=1.0` (keep amp up so you isolate the *filter* motion; sustain-stage cutoff stays audible).
- **Digeridoo drone:** **don't rely on bare filter self-oscillation** (Analog's filter needs an excitation impulse; undocumented). Use `OSC1 On Shape=Sine`, `F1 Type=LP24`, `F1 Res=0.85–0.95`, `F1 Freq=0.30–0.40`, `AEG1 S=1.0`, slow `LFO1→F1 Freq`. Play C1–C2.
- **Reese:** OSC1+OSC2 Saw, `OSC2 Detune≈+20c`, `F1 Freq=0.5`, `F1 Res=0.2`, `AEG1 S=1.0`. Play C1.
- **303:** mono+Glide, Saw, `F1 Type=LP12`, `F1 Res=0.75`, base `F1 Freq=0.35`, `F1 Freq<Env=+0.5`, `FEG1 D=0.35 S=0`, `F1 Drive=Sym`. Play C2.
- **A/B demos:** hold a fixed base patch (Saw, Balance 0.5, `F1 Freq=0.45`, `F1 Res=0.5`, AEG S=0.9, same note/vel), flip ONLY the one variable (`F1 Type` LP12↔LP24 / `F1 Drive` Off↔Sym / `Unison On/Off`). Pick `F1 Freq=0.45` so the slope difference is obvious.
