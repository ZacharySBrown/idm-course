The other half of FM's character lives in the ratio. Let r equal f-m divided by f-c. The spectrum has lines at f-c times one plus n-r. If r is rational — p over q in lowest terms — every line is an integer multiple of some fundamental. The spectrum is *harmonic* and the ear hears a pitched note. If r is irrational, the lines never line up. The spectrum is *inharmonic* and the ear hears bell, gong, metal, or noise.

Your ear performs an approximate harmonic-template match — only when sidebands fall on integer multiples of *some* fundamental does pitch fusion occur. For irrational r, the percept becomes object-like. Bell. Plate. Drum. Not note-like.
