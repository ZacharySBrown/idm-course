The other half of FM's character lives in the *ratio* between the carrier and modulator pitches. If the modulator is pitched at a whole-number multiple of the carrier — exactly twice, exactly three times — all the new tones land cleanly on the harmonic series, and your ear hears a *pitched note*. If the ratio is irrational — square-root-of-two, the golden ratio — the new tones fall *off* the harmonic grid. Your ear stops hearing a note and starts hearing an *object*. A bell. A plate. A drum.
