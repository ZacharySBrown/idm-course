Markus Popp and the others formed Oval in Berlin in nineteen-ninety-one, and their instrument was a damaged compact disc. Popp scratched CDs. He drew on the underside with markers. He taped them to force the player to skip and loop. He took, in the documented phrasing, "playback malfunctions" and turned them into "deliberate musical elements" — most famously on *Systemisch*, nineteen-ninety-four, on Mille Plateaux. Listen to what a skipping, error-laden CD sounds like when the error is the instrument.
