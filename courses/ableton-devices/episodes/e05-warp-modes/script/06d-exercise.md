Homework for the walk home.

[pause 500ms]

Pick the most boring two seconds of audio you own. A vocal "ah." A snare. A single chord. Do not add a single plugin. Just warp it.

[pause 400ms]

Put it in Texture and turn the Flux up until the pitch dissolves into a cloud. Put it in Beats, Loop-Forward it into a stutter. Put it in Re-Pitch and drop it to half speed for the dark tape grain. Put it in Complex Pro and pull the formants out until it is a goblin.

[pause 400ms]

Now make forty seconds of music where every sound is the same two seconds, broken six different ways. Same source. Six breakages. Nothing else.

[pause 600ms]

If you can do that, you have understood that warping was never about tempo. Gabor's grains, Xenakis's razor blade, Flanagan's phase vocoder, the lab in Paris, the Bieber meme — they all collapse into one dropdown, and the whole instrument is the decision about how you let the sound fall apart.

[pause 500ms]

Pick the breakage.

[pause 800ms]

That's *Warp*.
