Marc Leclair, working as Akufen, made *My Way* in two-thousand-two, and it more or less defined microhouse. The method: micro-samples of FM radio — in the documented description, "brief slivers of songs, commercials, DJs' banter and static, most often less than a second long" — reassembled "like a puzzle or collage." Ableton's own artist interview confirms it. The origin is even more literal: as a kid he ran a double tape recorder, pause-record-pause off the radio for hours.
