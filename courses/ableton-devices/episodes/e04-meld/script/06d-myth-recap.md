One. Meld is not a physical modeller. It's a macro-oscillator synth — twenty-five pre-built oscillator algorithms behind two knobs each. The only physical-modelling DSP in the whole instrument is two filter types, the Plate and Membrane resonators. It's a filter, not the oscillator. That's the honest version of the hybrid label on the box.

[pause 500ms]

Two. Meld did not arrive in 12.1. It shipped with Live 12.0, the fifth of March, twenty-twenty-four, alongside Roar and Granulator III. The only thing added afterward was the Chord oscillator, in 12.2. If you read 12.1 somewhere, it was wrong — I had it wrong too at the top of this.
