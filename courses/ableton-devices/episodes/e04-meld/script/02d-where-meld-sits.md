So where does Meld sit, against the other three synths we've covered. Four instruments, four verbs.

[pause 500ms]

Operator *generates* — it builds a spectrum from sine operators with FM. That was episode one. Wavetable *scans* — it interpolates across a position in a table. That was episode three. Analog *subtracts* — it carves a waveform with filters. And Meld *picks-and-plays* — you choose a finished algorithm and route your fingers into it. Same family, four different ways into a sound. The clean version for a physicist: Operator generates spectra, Wavetable interpolates between them, Meld parameterizes whole algorithms behind two macros and lets ten fingers drive them.
