There's another coast. Don Buchla, in Berkeley, built instruments with no keyboard — touch plates and sequencers instead. And the philosophy is inverted: you start *simple*, with sine waves, and *add* complexity, with waveshaping and wavefolding and complex oscillators cross-modulating each other. The signature Buchla circuit is the low-pass gate — a filter and an amplifier fused into one plucky, vactrol-driven module that opens and closes together.
