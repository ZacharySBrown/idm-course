Homework for the walk home.

[pause 500ms]

Open Wavetable. Pick the most boring table you can find — Basic Shapes. Hold one note and do nothing but modulate Position. An envelope, an LFO, your mod wheel, your finger pressure. Pick one, or stack them.

[pause 400ms]

No new notes. No filter sweeps. Make forty seconds of music where every change in the sound is a change in which waveform you're playing.

[pause 600ms]

If you can do it, you've understood Wolfgang Palm's accident from nineteen-seventy-eight — that you don't have to filter a sound to make it move. You can walk between sounds instead. Same idea from the PPG choir to the Skrillex growl. Only the rate of the walk is different.

[pause 600ms]

Pitch and timbre, separate dials. Walk the timbre.

[pause 800ms]
